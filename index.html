Privacy Policy

Asan Brahui Tarjuma Quran e Karim (آسان براہوئی ترجمہ قرآن کریم)
Last updated: October 7, 2026
Key Summary: This application is completely offline and does NOT collect, transmit, track, or share any personal information. All your reading progress and bookmarks remain entirely on your own device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Asan Brahui Tarjuma Quran e Karim (Package: com.brahuiquran). We deeply respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This policy explains our handling of any data when you use our app.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information.

When using this app:

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account details.
  • We do not track your precise or approximate location.
  • We do not access your contacts, camera, photos, microphone, or device storage files.
  • We do not collect unique device identifiers (IMEI, MAC address, Advertising IDs).

3. Offline Storage & Local Data

The Holy Quran text, Brahui translation, and Tafseer are bundled locally inside the application database. The app functions 100% offline without requiring an internet connection.

Certain app preferences and user actions are saved strictly on your local device:

  • Bookmarks: Ayahs you choose to bookmark.
  • Last Read Position: The last Surah, Para, or Ayah you read to allow easy resuming.
  • Display Settings: Dark/light mode theme selection and font scaling sizes.

This data is stored solely in local device storage (SQLite database and local device storage). It is never uploaded to any remote server or shared with third parties.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

  • No Advertisements: This application contains zero ads. We do not integrate any ad networks (such as Google AdMob, Unity, or Facebook Audience Network).
  • No Tracking Analytics: We do not use third-party user tracking SDKs (such as Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, or Mixpanel).

5. Permissions

The application does not request any sensitive Android permissions. Standard system capabilities (such as clipboard access when you deliberately tap "Copy Ayah" or standard system share when you tap "Share") are handled natively by your operating system only upon explicit user action.

6. Children's Privacy

Our app is designed for people of all ages, including children, for educational and religious reading purposes. Because we collect no personal information whatsoever from anyone, we are fully compliant with children's privacy regulations, including the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policy.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy. Any updates will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please feel free to contact the developer:

  • Email: support@brahuiquran.com (or your personal developer email)
  • App: Asan Brahui Tarjuma Quran e Karim
